NanoVDB:
  Improvements:
  - tools::cuda::TopologyBuilder stores a lower-node mask only for each upper-node
    child the consumer's internal-node functors touch, instead of a full row of
    32,768 lower masks (16 MiB) per speculative root tile. Scratch now scales with
    the output topology rather than the tile count; outputs are byte-identical.
    A batch of 64 small grids that touch ijk = 0 dilates with 80 MiB of device
    memory instead of 8.4 GiB and 3.8x faster; large grids build at the same speed
    with 1.2-19x lower peak device memory. RefineGrid, CoarsenGrid, DilateGrid,
    PruneGrid, MergeGrids and MeshToGrid use the new path; MeshToGrid first marks
    the upper-node children its triangles touch, so its pool is sized exactly.
    Fixes #2392.

  API Changes:
  - Added TopologyBuilder::buildInternalMasks(stream, maxRows, launch), which calls
    launch(upperMasks, lowerMasks) once with a util::morphology::cuda::SparseLowerMasks
    view, plus allocateLowerMasks and lowerMasks() for callers that launch the
    functors themselves. maxRows bounds the (tile, upper child) pairs the functors
    touch, and TopologyBuilder::UnknownMaxRows reserves a row for every pair;
    exceeding the bound makes getBuffer and waitForNodeCounts throw. Kernels that
    write through SparseLowerMasks, and so the CUDA topology tools, require compute
    capability 7.0 or newer; the deprecated dense path does not.
  - The internal-node functors in nanovdb/util/cuda/Morphology.cuh (refine, coarsen,
    dilate, prune and merge) take any lower-mask view from the new header
    nanovdb/util/cuda/LowerMasks.cuh. RasterizeInternalNodesFunctor takes the view
    type as a third template parameter, defaulting to DenseLowerMasks, which converts
    from the raw array.
  - Deprecated TopologyBuilder::allocateInternalMaskBuffers, deviceLowerMasks,
    lowerOffsetRows and leafOffsetRows, and the functor overloads that take a raw
    Mask<4>(*)[Mask<5>::SIZE] array. They keep working until the next major release;
    the dense accessors throw std::logic_error after buildInternalMasks.
