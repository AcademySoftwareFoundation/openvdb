NanoVDB:

  New features:
  - tools::cuda::voxelsToGrid has a batched overload that builds one grid per segment of a single device voxel list, given device offsets, into one buffer. The whole batch waits on the stream a fixed number of times, however many grids it builds, and holds at most 2^32-1 voxels.

  Improvements:
  - The voxel-set overloads of tools::cuda::voxelsToGrid build all sets in one pass instead of building each set and merging the results; Point grids are still built one set at a time. Output grids are unchanged, but the sets together may hold at most 2^32-1 voxels.
