NanoVDB:

  Improvements:
  - tools::cuda::ConnectedComponents is faster, with the same interface and the same labels. The per-leaf
    union-find runs over the z-runs of a leaf instead of its voxels, with one warp per leaf; components that touch
    across a leaf face are united where they are found, without an intermediate edge list; and each component's
    label is read once and written at all of its voxels.
