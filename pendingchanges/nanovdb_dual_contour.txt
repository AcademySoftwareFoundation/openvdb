NanoVDB:

  New Features:
  - Added nanovdb/math/Primitives.h with math::Triangle, which tools::cuda::Triangle
    now aliases, and the non-owning indexed-mesh views math::TriangleMeshView and
    math::TriangleMeshBatchView. tools::cuda::MeshToGrid takes a TriangleMeshView in an
    added constructor.
  - Added nanovdb::tools::cuda::DualContour, a CUDA dual-contouring (QEF) triangle-mesh
    extraction from a scalar field (e.g. a signed distance field) on the active voxels of
    ValueOnIndex grids. It meshes one grid, or a batch of grids together through a batched
    VoxelBlockManager with a constant number of host synchronizations per chunk of grids.
    Optional cluster-collapse decimation, uniform (setReduce) or curvature-adaptive
    (setAdaptivity), never merges vertices of different grids. Without decimation each
    grid's mesh is bitwise identical to meshing it alone; decimation sums its QEFs with
    atomics, so its vertex positions can differ in their last bits from run to run. Added an
    ex_dual_contour_cuda example and unit tests.
