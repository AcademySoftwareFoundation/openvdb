NanoVDB:

  API Changes:
  - Removed the forwarding headers deprecated since OpenVDB 12.0 when NanoVDB reorganized its headers into math/, tools/ and io/: nanovdb/util/CpuTimer.h, CreateNanoGrid.h, DitherLUT.h, GridBuilder.h, GridChecksum.h, GridStats.h, GridValidator.h, HDDA.h, HostBuffer.h, IO.h, NanoToOpenVDB.h, NodeManager.h, OpenToNanoVDB.h, Primitives.h, Ray.h, SampleFromVoxels.h and Stencils.h, and nanovdb/util/cuda/CudaAddBlindData.cuh, CudaGridChecksum.cuh, CudaGridHandle.cuh, CudaGridStats.cuh, CudaGridValidator.cuh, CudaIndexToGrid.cuh, CudaNodeManager.cuh, CudaPointsToGrid.cuh, CudaSignedFloodFill.cuh, CudaUtils.h and GpuTimer.h. Include the headers they named instead.
  - Removed the forms deprecated since OpenVDB 12.0 in favor of the namespaced API: nanovdb::cudaPointsToGrid, cudaVoxelsToGrid, cudaIndexToGrid, cudaCreateNanoGrid, cudaAddBlindData, cudaSignedFloodFill, cudaGridStats, cudaIsValid and cudaLambdaKernel (use the nanovdb::tools::cuda and nanovdb::util::cuda functions); the aliases nanovdb::Ray, Rgba8, BBoxR, CpuTimer, GpuTimer, Range1D, Range2D and Range3D (use the nanovdb::math and nanovdb::util types); nanovdb::forEach, reduce, invoke, prefixSum, FindLowestOn, FindHighestOn and CountOn (use the nanovdb::util functions); nanovdb::checkGrid, nanovdb::isValid(const NanoGrid*, CheckMode, bool), nanovdb::nanoToOpenVDB and nanovdb::createSampler (use the nanovdb::tools and nanovdb::math functions); tools::checksum (use tools::evalChecksum); tools::isValid(const NanoGrid&, bool detailed, bool) (pass a pointer and a CheckMode); the forms of tools::validateChecksum and tools::updateChecksum that take a grid by reference (pass a pointer); and tools::gridStats (use tools::updateGridStats); mapToGridType and mapToGridClass (use toGridType and toGridClass); Checksum::checksum and Checksum::isPartial (use full, head, tail and isHalf); and Grid::blindData (use Grid::getBlindData).
  - Removed GridHandle::size, deprecated since OpenVDB 12.1; use GridHandle::bufferSize.
  - Removed math::ZeroCrossing and math::ZeroCrossingNode, deprecated since OpenVDB 13.1; use math::zeroCrossing and math::zeroCrossingNode.
  - nanovdb/cmd/updateFiles.sh and updateFiles.py now rewrite every removed header and the renamed functions above. Calls that must change from a grid reference to a pointer, calls to cudaPointsToGrid that relied on its default voxel size, and the removed members (GridHandle::size, Grid::blindData, Checksum::checksum) still need a manual edit, as does nanovdb::isValid, whose name the scripts can't tell apart from the nanovdb::isValid(GridType, GridClass) that remains.
