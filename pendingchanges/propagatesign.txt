NanoVDB:
  New Features:
  - Added tools::cuda::PropagateSign, the index-grid counterpart of tools::cuda::signedFloodFill: given the
    SDF values of the active voxels of a ValueOnIndex grid, it fills caller-allocated masks per level (leaf,
    lower, upper) and returns the root-tile origins of the inactive regions that are inside, without modifying
    the grid.
